## Pipeline (`pipeline/` at the project root, Node/TypeScript)
1. **Lead input:** `leads/{slug}/listing.json` holds the raw DataForSEO listing (name, phone, address, coordinates, rating, reviews, hours, attributes). Slugs come from the business name, de-duplicated.
2. **Assemble the content JSON:**
   - Mechanical, no LLM: business details, hours, rating, the 5 best reviews word for word, theme (rotated across the batch), and the 6 starter-pack services.
   - LLM: only the 6 areas (essays and FAQs), hero line, story and about text, and meta titles.
3. **Ground the area pages in a fact file:** add `atlanta-areas.json` with about 25 metro cities (county, neighborhoods, housing eras, coordinates). The featured area is the lead's own city; the other 5 are the nearest from the list. The LLM writes only from those facts.
4. **Validate and retry:** parse every JSON with the template's zod schema (`templates/cleaning/src/content-schema.ts`). Send any errors back to the LLM, with up to 2 retries. `z.toJSONSchema` gives the structured-output schema, so there is one contract.
5. **Build:** `SITE_DATA=leads/{slug}/site.json astro build --outDir sites/{slug}`, then `kai-check` on each lead. A failure stops that lead.
6. **Deploy:** one Cloudflare Worker with static assets serves everything, using a wildcard route `*.callcatcher.site/*` that maps the subdomain to its folder. One deploy covers all 50.
7. **Write back:** the preview URL goes to column F of Calls Template → `scrapes`, via the Google Sheets connector.
8. **Spot-check:** a human skims 3–5 sites before calls start, especially the area essays.

## Defaults assumed
- **Model:** Sonnet 5.5, with the 50 leads sent as one batch. Measure cost per lead during the run.
- **Photos:** stock only for v1. Skip Maps `main_image`.
- **Claim button:** one Stripe Payment Link for every site, with `?client_reference_id={slug}`, created before deploy.
